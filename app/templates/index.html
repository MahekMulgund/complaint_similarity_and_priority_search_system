Key System Capabilities

Engineered to eliminate duplicate tickets, prioritize emergencies objectively, and provide complete resolution transparency across campus departments.

Submission & Tracking

Easy-to-use complaint intake form with location tagging, category mapping, severity, and impacted user counts. Instant reference IDs allow complainants to track their issue in real time.

NLP Similarity Detection

Uses NLTK text preprocessing, TF-IDF representations, and Cosine Similarity to identify duplicate reports and automatically group recurring issues into unified clusters.

Automatic Priority Ranking

Employs an objective 4-factor formula (recurring count, severity, affected population, recency) to compute a normalized score and classify issues into Low, Medium, High, or Critical.

Status Monitoring & Audit

Maintains clear separation between queue processing states and departmental resolution stages. Every status transition records actor, timestamp, and resolution remarks.
